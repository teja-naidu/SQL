# E-Commerce Sales and Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using SQL to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, marketing effectiveness, and revenue trends.

The objective is to demonstrate how SQL can be used to solve real-world business problems by transforming raw transactional data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision making.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains **1,200 e-commerce orders** collected between **January 2023 and June 2025**.

### Dataset Includes

* Order Details
* Customer Information
* Product Information
* Quantity Purchased
* Unit Price
* Total Revenue
* Payment Method
* Coupon Code
* Referral Source
* Order Status

---

## Business Objectives

This project focuses on answering key business questions, including:

* How many orders and customers does the business have?
* What is the total revenue and average order value?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which marketing channels perform best?
* How effective are coupon campaigns?
* What are the customer payment preferences?
* How does revenue change over time?
