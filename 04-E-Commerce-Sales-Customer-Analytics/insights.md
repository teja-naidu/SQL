# Day 1 Insights - Sales Overview Analytics

## Dataset Overview

* Total Orders: 1,200
* Total Customers: 1,189
* Total Revenue: $1,264,761.96
* Average Order Value: $1,053.97
* Analysis Period: January 2023 to June 2025

## Key Findings

### Order Performance

* The dataset contains 1,200 e-commerce orders during the analysis period.
* A total of 1,189 unique customers placed orders on the platform.
* The small difference between total orders and total customers indicates that most customers placed only one order.

### Revenue Analysis

* The business generated total revenue of $1.26 million.
* Average Order Value (AOV) is approximately $1,053.97.
* The high AOV suggests customers frequently purchase medium to high-value products.

### Monthly Revenue Trends

* Revenue fluctuated across different months, indicating varying customer purchasing behavior.
* May 2023 generated approximately $63.8K in revenue.
* June 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $68.1K.
* Revenue remained consistent throughout the observed period, demonstrating stable sales performance.

### Customer Base Analysis

* The business successfully acquired a large number of unique customers.
* Repeat purchases appear limited based on the close relationship between total orders and unique customers.
* Customer retention initiatives could help increase repeat sales and long-term revenue growth.

## Business Value

Sales overview analytics help businesses:

* Monitor overall business performance.
* Track revenue growth over time.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Measure customer acquisition effectiveness.
* Support strategic planning and forecasting decisions.
