# Movie & Streaming Platform Analytics - Insights

## Dataset Overview

- Total Movies: 449
- Analysis Period: 1920 – 2025
- Total Languages: 27
- Total Production Companies: 123
- Streaming Provider Combinations: 80

---

# Key Findings

## Movie Collection

- The dataset contains 449 highly-rated movies ranked using a combination of critic and audience scores.
- The collection spans over 105 years of cinema history, from 1920 to 2025.
- Movies are available in 27 different languages, representing global cinema across multiple countries.

## Ratings Analysis

- The average custom movie score is **84.23**, indicating consistently high-quality films throughout the dataset.
- The average IMDb rating is **7.97/10**, reflecting strong audience reception.
- The highest IMDb rating is **9.2/10**.
- The highest Flickmetrix score recorded is **93.0**.

## Audience Popularity

- Movies have an average of **431,523 IMDb votes**, demonstrating substantial audience engagement.
- Only **5 movies** have IMDb ratings of **9.0 or above**, making them exceptionally rare.
- Nearly the entire dataset consists of movies rated between **7.0 and 8.9**, highlighting the consistently high quality of selected films.

## Decade Analysis

- The **2010s** contain the largest number of movies (**115**), followed by the **2000s** (**85**).
- The dataset contains relatively fewer classic films from the 1920s through the 1950s.
- The average movie release year is **1994**, indicating a balanced mix of classic and modern cinema.

## Production Companies

- The dataset includes movies from **123 production companies**.
- Warner Bros. Pictures contributes the highest number of identified movies (**24**), followed by Paramount Pictures (**21**) and Sony Pictures Classics (**19**).
- Around **120 movies** have missing production company information.

## Language Distribution

- English dominates the dataset with **334 movies**.
- Japanese (**21**) and French (**20**) are the next most represented languages.
- Spanish, Italian, German, Korean, Chinese, Danish, and Turkish films further increase international diversity.

## Streaming Availability

- Movies are available across **80 unique streaming provider combinations**.
- **362 movies** are available on multiple streaming platforms, indicating broad digital distribution and accessibility.

# Day 2 Insights - Movie Ratings & Genre Analytics

## Key Findings

### Highest Rated Movies

- **The Godfather (1972)** ranks as the highest-rated movie in the dataset with a **Custom Score of 93.83**.
- Other top-performing movies include **Seven Samurai**, **12 Angry Men**, **Schindler's List**, and **The Godfather: Part II**.
- These films consistently receive exceptional ratings across IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, and audience reviews.

### IMDb Ratings

- **The Godfather** holds the highest IMDb rating of **9.2/10**.
- Five movies have IMDb ratings of **9.0 or higher**, making them exceptionally rare among the 449 selected films.
- Highly rated classics such as **The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King**, **Schindler's List**, and **12 Angry Men** continue to maintain outstanding audience appreciation.

### Movie Popularity

- **Inception** is the most popular movie based on IMDb votes, receiving more than **2.66 million votes**.
- **Fight Club**, **Forrest Gump**, **Pulp Fiction**, and **Interstellar** also rank among the most widely watched and reviewed films.
- Popularity does not always correspond directly with the highest critic ratings, demonstrating differences between audience reach and critical acclaim.

### Genre Distribution

- **Drama** is the most represented genre with **56 movies**.
- Drama-related combinations such as **Drama, Romance**, **Drama, Crime**, and **Comedy, Drama** dominate the dataset.
- Documentary and historical dramas also have strong representation.

### Genre Performance

- **Adventure, Fantasy, Action** achieves the highest average IMDb rating (**8.90**) and the highest average Custom Score (**88.39**).
- **Drama, History, War** records the highest average audience rating (**90.60**).
- Crime, thriller, western, and historical drama genres consistently achieve above-average ratings across multiple review platforms.

### Audience Ratings

- **The Godfather** and **Wolfwalkers** receive the highest audience rating of **95**.
- Several classics including **Seven Samurai**, **Schindler's List**, and **Goodfellas** maintain audience ratings above **94**, demonstrating long-term popularity.

### Critic Ratings

- **Citizen Kane** leads Rotten Tomatoes critic ratings with a score of **98**.
- Several legendary films including **Seven Samurai**, **Casablanca**, **Lawrence of Arabia**, and **Parasite** receive critic ratings above **95**.
- These movies continue to be recognized as benchmark films in cinematic history.

### Metacritic Analysis

- Seven movies achieve a perfect **100 Metacritic score**, including **The Godfather**, **Citizen Kane**, **Rear Window**, and **Lawrence of Arabia**.
- Classic cinema dominates the highest Metacritic rankings, reflecting strong critical consensus over multiple decades.

The analysis demonstrates how different rating platforms evaluate movies from distinct perspectives.

These insights can help streaming platforms:

- Recommend highly rated movies to users.
- Balance audience favorites with critically acclaimed films.

# Day 4 Insights - Streaming Platform & Audience Analytics

## Key Findings

### Streaming Platform Availability

- The most common streaming provider combination is **iTunes + Amazon Video**, covering **134 movies**.
- **iTunes + Amazon Video + Max** appears for **51 movies**, while **Prime Video + iTunes + Amazon Video** includes **31 movies**.
- More than **362 movies** are available on multiple streaming platforms, providing viewers with greater accessibility.

### Streaming Quality Analysis

- Movies available on **HBO + iTunes + Amazon Video + Max** have the highest average Custom Score (**88.85**).
- Streaming combinations including **Paramount+**, **Prime Video**, and **Max** also maintain above-average movie quality.
- Popular streaming services consistently host critically acclaimed and audience-favorite films.

### Audience vs Critic Preferences

- **Fight Club** shows the largest positive difference between audience and critic ratings, with audiences rating it **21 points higher** than critics.
- **Se7en**, **Casino**, **Oldboy**, and **Dead Poets Society** also receive significantly stronger audience appreciation.
- These results demonstrate that audience favorites are not always the highest-rated films among professional critics.

### Critics vs Audience Preferences

- **Uncut Gems** has the largest critic advantage, receiving **29 points higher** from critics than audiences.
- **Pinocchio (1940)**, **E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial**, **Roma**, and **Boyhood** also receive considerably stronger critic scores.
- This highlights the differences between critical evaluation and mainstream audience reception.

### Rating Platform Comparison

Average ratings across review platforms:

- IMDb Rating: **7.97 / 10**
- Metacritic: **86.75**
- Rotten Tomatoes Critics: **85.58**
- Rotten Tomatoes Audience: **85.75**
- Custom Score: **84.23**

The relatively small variation indicates strong consistency across major review platforms.

### IMDb Popularity Analysis

- **203 movies** have between **100K and 500K IMDb votes**, representing the largest popularity category.
- Only **9 movies** have surpassed **2 million IMDb votes**, making them exceptionally popular worldwide.
- The dataset balances blockbuster films with critically acclaimed niche cinema.

### Audience Favorites

- **The Godfather** and **Wolfwalkers** share the highest audience rating (**95**).
- Other audience favorites include **Schindler's List**, **Goodfellas**, **12 Angry Men**, and **Seven Samurai**.
- These movies continue to receive exceptional appreciation across generations.

### Critically Acclaimed Movies

- **Citizen Kane**, **The Godfather**, **Casablanca**, **Lawrence of Arabia**, and **Rear Window** dominate critic-based rankings.
- Several classic films achieve perfect **100 Metacritic** scores, demonstrating long-term critical recognition.

### Highly Rated Blockbusters

Among movies with more than **1 million IMDb votes**, the highest Custom Scores belong to:

- The Godfather
- Schindler's List
- The Godfather: Part II
- Pulp Fiction
- Parasite

These films successfully combine massive popularity with exceptional critical acclaim.
