# Food Delivery Operations Analytics using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes food delivery operations data using SQL and DuckDB to uncover meaningful business insights related to customer behavior, revenue generation, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and business KPIs.

The analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw operational data into actionable insights through data exploration, aggregations, window functions, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and business reporting.

---

# Dataset Overview

The dataset contains approximately **15,000 food delivery orders** with information related to:

- Customer demographics
- Order details
- Delivery performance
- Restaurant ratings
- Delivery partner ratings
- Customer ratings
- Discounts and promotions
- Premium customer information
- Festival and weekend orders
- Delivery efficiency metrics
- Cancellation and refund details

# Key Business Insights

- Processed **15,000** food delivery orders.
- Generated approximately **$1.79 Million** in total revenue.
- Average order value was **$119.08**.
- Premium customers contributed over **30%** of total revenue while maintaining higher average spending per order.
- City Tier 3 generated the highest overall revenue.
- Customers aged **55 years and above** contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Delivery delay rate remained below **10%**.
- Cancellation rate was approximately **13.35%**.
- Refund rate remained around **4.12%**.
- Customer satisfaction averaged **3.99/5**, while restaurant and delivery partner ratings consistently exceeded **4/5**.
- Revenue remained stable throughout the year with only minor monthly fluctuations.
